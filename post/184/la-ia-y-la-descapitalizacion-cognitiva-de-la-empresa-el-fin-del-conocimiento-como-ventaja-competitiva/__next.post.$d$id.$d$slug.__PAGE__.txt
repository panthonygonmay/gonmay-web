The axis must shift: from an economy of resources to one of decisions. Frank Knight already distinguished in 1921 between risk (quantifiable and insurable) and uncertainty (the unknown, that which has no past). While industrial economics managed risk and physical resources, the AI economy moves toward reducing uncertainty. AI doesn't produce "things"; it produces predictions. And when prediction becomes a cheap commodity, efficiency ceases to be a competitive advantage and becomes mere entry cost.
