La empresa industrial dependía de proveedores de maquinaria, pero podía cambiar de proveedor si era necesario. Esta dependencia es más profunda porque afecta a la capacidad de la empresa para generar conocimiento, no solo para producir bienes. La empresa no puede simplemente cambiar de proveedor de IA como cambiaría de proveedor de servidores; la inferencia que produce el sistema está incrustada en los procesos de decisión de la empresa, y cambiar de sistema implica reentrenar modelos, reconfigurar procesos y, en muchos casos, perder el conocimiento acumulado. The firm becomes a node in a cognitive network it does not control. It's comfortable while the network works; it's a trap when the network fails. The result is an epistemic dependency fundamentally different from the vendor lock-in that characterized the industrial firm. The industrial firm depended on machinery suppliers but could switch providers if needed. The post-AI firm depends on providers of cognitive capacity, and switching suppliers means not just changing software but transferring the knowledge base the firm has accumulated in the system. This dependency is deeper because it affects the firm's ability to generate knowledge, not just to produce goods. The firm cannot simply swap AI providers like it would swap server vendors; the inference produced by the system is embedded in the firm's decision-making processes, and changing systems means retraining models, reconfiguring processes, and in many cases losing accumulated knowledge.
