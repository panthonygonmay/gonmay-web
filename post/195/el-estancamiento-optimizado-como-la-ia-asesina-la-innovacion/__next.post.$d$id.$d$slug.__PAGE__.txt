En ese escenario, el desacople no es un fenómeno marginal o transitorio, sino el nuevo estado estable de la economía. La economía industrial podía permitirse ser ineficiente con los humanos porque necesitaba su capacidad cognitiva. But if AI matches or exceeds human performance in a growing range of cognitive tasks, the premise of an ever-present 'refuge' for human employment weakens. AI doesn't need to beat humans in all tasks; it only needs to beat them in enough tasks that the opportunity cost of employing humans becomes prohibitive for most firms. In that scenario, decoupling is not marginal or transitory, but the new steady state of the economy. The industrial economy could afford to be inefficient with humans because it needed their cognitive capacity.
