If efficiency is total and cost is zero, innovation stagnates. Here emerges the "Intention Economy." Economic activity has historically centered on execution: paying a programmer to write code or an accountant to balance books. However, AI has turned execution into a generic commodity. When the "how" is free, value violently migrates to the "what" and the "why." In photography, the value of technical execution—developing a negative or adjusting exposure—fell to zero with digitalization and AI. Yet, artistic vision, the ability to decide which moment to capture, was enhanced. The professional of the future will not master the tool but will define the strategic objective and exercise critical judgment over the result. Value no longer lies in processing information but in giving it meaning. This transition redefines rent: if productivity increases but human execution value disappears, the distribution gap becomes unsustainable. Human labor is no longer the limiting factor in production, so productivity does not automatically translate into wages.
